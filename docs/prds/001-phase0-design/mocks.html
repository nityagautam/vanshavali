Vanshavali — design direction “Old paper record”

Phase 0 mocks for PRD 001. Aged-paper surfaces with ink-coloured accents, in light and dark. Switch between three paper palettes, light/dark, and Admin/Viewer (viewers never see Add, Edit or Move). Static illustrations, not the live app.

1 · Phone

Search is the primary control. Controls live in the bottom third. Detail and filters are sheets, not drawers. “Add” is the one filled button and is shown to admins only.
